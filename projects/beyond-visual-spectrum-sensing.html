Project

Beyond Visual Spectrum Sensing.

Project Overview

As part of my robotics research, I am investigating how thermal, depth, and LiDAR sensing can be integrated with the hardware and software of MBot mobile robots. The goal is to expand the robot's perception capabilities beyond conventional RGB-based sensing and evaluate how different sensing modalities can complement one another in robotic environments.

My work involves researching feasible sensors and associated hardware, adapting the MBot platform to support new sensing configurations, and developing the software necessary to collect and utilize the resulting sensor data. I am also helping translate my findings into course content and educational materials, allowing students to explore multimodal perception and sensor integration through hands-on robotics projects.

Key Technical Aspects

  • Multimodal Perception: Investigating combinations of thermal imaging, depth sensing, and LiDAR for robotic perception.
  • Thermal Vision: Researching thermal cameras, thermography, and methods for extracting information from infrared imagery.
  • LiDAR: Exploring LiDAR-based ranging and spatial mapping capabilities for mobile robots.
  • Hardware Integration: Investigating how sensors and associated hardware can be physically and electrically integrated with the mBot platform.
  • Embedded/Robotics Software: Adapting existing robot software to accommodate additional sensing hardware and data streams.
  • Sensor Fusion: Exploring how complementary sensing modalities can be combined to provide more robust environmental information.
  • Robotics Education: Translating research findings into instructional material and course content for students working with mobile robots.
